# Example plan

This is a method sample. When the user names a different war, throw away every name and coordinate below and plan from their prompt.

## Sample prompt

"About 50 seconds on Chandragupta Maurya and Seleucus Nicator. Show both armies moving forward, then the treaty that hands over the northwestern lands. I will drop square portraits in later."

## What you do not assume

Classical accounts (Strabo, Appian, and the Justin epitome) report a war around 305 BCE and a treaty. Modern books often name Arachosia, Gedrosia, and the Paropamisadae among the ceded lands, and they repeat a gift of elephants. The exact list is debated. Before you color a region, cite the sentence you are following. A Mauryan statue is not a certified likeness of Chandragupta. Seleucus can be a coin portrait. Anything else is labeled `artistic reconstruction` or left as initials.
